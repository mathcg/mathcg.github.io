About me

About Me

I am a Research Scientist at Meta. I develop foundational models to combat scams and dangerous content, including suicide and self-injury content, and help protect child safety across Facebook and Instagram.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Washington in 2022, where I was fortunate to be advised by Professor Yen-Chi Chen. My Ph.D. work sits at the intersection of statistics and machine learning, with an emphasis on rigorous inference from complex or incomplete data.

Research interests

My research interests include:

  • Nonparametric inference and bootstrap confidence bands,
  • Missing data and robust estimation,
  • Clinical trial and observational data integration,
  • Statistical machine learning and uncertainty quantification.
