About me

Hello, I’m Gang.

I am a Research Scientist at Meta. My work sits at the intersection of statistics and machine learning, with an emphasis on rigorous inference from complex or incomplete data.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Washington in 2022, where I was fortunate to be advised by Professor Yen-Chi Chen. My dissertation developed missing-data methods motivated by observational health datasets.

Research interests

01

Nonparametric inference

Bootstrap confidence bands and debiased estimators for density and regression problems.

02

Missing data

Identification and robust estimation under complex, nonmonotone, and nonignorable missingness.

03

Data integration

Combining clinical trials with observational follow-up data to estimate long-term effects.

04

Statistical machine learning

Principled methodology for reliable learning and uncertainty quantification.
