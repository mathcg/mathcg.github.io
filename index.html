01 About

Building methods for inference when data are complex or incomplete.

I am a Research Scientist at Meta. My academic work develops principled statistical methods for uncertainty quantification, incomplete data, and the integration of experimental and observational evidence.

I earned my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Washington in 2022, advised by Yen-Chi Chen. My dissertation focused on missing-data methods motivated by observational health datasets.
